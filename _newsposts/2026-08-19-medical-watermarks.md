[AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/llm-watermarks-degrade-medical-reasoning-eth-paper-finds) covered our study [Marking the Wrong Symptoms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20462), by Melanie Rieff, Robin Staab, Thibaud Gloaguen, Stefan Hegselmann and Martin Vechev. The study examines how text watermarking can affect medical reasoning in language and vision-language models.
